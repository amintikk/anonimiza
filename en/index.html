Remove personal data from a text before you pass it to an AI

Paste a text or open a PDF or DOCX. Anonimiza replaces names, ID numbers, IBANs, phone numbers or addresses with markers such as [PERSON_1], and when the AI replies it puts each item back. Everything happens in your browser: the text is not sent anywhere.

How it works

  1. Detect

    It finds the personal data in the text and highlights it. No account, nothing to install.

  2. Review

    Remove anything that is not personal data and select anything missing. The same item always gets the same marker.

  3. Use and restore

    Copy the text, or download the PDF or DOCX with the same layout, and use it with any AI. Paste its reply into “Restore” to get the data back.

What it detects

Names, organisations, addresses, Spanish DNI, NIE, CIF and social security numbers, IBANs, cards, phone numbers, emails, IP addresses, dates of birth, number plates and case, medical record, policy or user numbers. Anything it misses you can mark yourself.

Limits

It does not guarantee complete anonymisation: always check the result before sharing it.

  • It sometimes gets it wrong. Mostly with lower-case names, chat aliases and codes without a word in front saying what they are.
  • Context identifies too. A job title, a place or a rare illness may be enough to know who is meant, and those are not looked for.
  • Images are not checked. Signatures, photos or stamps in a PDF or DOCX. Scanned PDFs have no text and cannot be read.
  • The anonymised PDF is one image per page. That way the original text is not left hidden underneath, but its text cannot be selected.
  • A 370 MB download the first time. The model then stays in the browser. On modest computers or phones a long document can take minutes.
  • Restoring only works in the same tab. Close it and the data to restore is gone.

Privacy

Your texts and files do not leave your device: everything is processed in the browser. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics. The code is open source on GitHub. Details in the privacy policy.

Questions

Why markers and not made-up data?

A marker such as [PERSON_1] makes clear what was removed, the AI keeps it, and it can be undone without ambiguity. A fake name could slip into the reply without you noticing.

What if the AI changes a marker?

Restoring tolerates the usual changes (case, spaces, lost brackets) and flags the ones it does not recognise.

Does it make me GDPR compliant?

It helps you share less data, but it is not a certified tool and does not replace a review. A text with markers is still, in general, pseudonymised data.
