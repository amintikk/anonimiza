Summary: your texts and files do not reach any server. They are processed in your browser. There are no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. The only personal data that reaches our systems is the technical data that comes with any visit to a website, such as your IP address.

What does not reach our systems

  • The texts you paste and the files you open. They are read, analysed and transformed in your browser. They are not uploaded to any site.
  • The replacement table. It lives only in the memory of the tab and disappears when you reload or close it. It is not saved, not even in your browser.
  • Analytics. We do not use Google Analytics or similar tools. The typeface is served from this same domain.

This is enforced by the browser itself: the page is served with a content security policy (CSP) that only allows connections to this site and to anonimiza-models.aminox.dev, from which the model is downloaded.

Providers involved

  • Oracle Cloud (server in Madrid, EU): hosts the website files.

If you use the result in another service

When you copy the anonymised text and paste it into an AI assistant or another service, that service processes it under its own terms. Anonimiza is not involved in that communication. Check that the text contains no personal data before doing so.

Storage in your browser

This site does not install cookies. It does store the following in your browser, without sending them to any server:

  • Preferences (localStorage): theme and sensitivity.
  • Downloaded model (Cache Storage), so that you do not have to download it every time.

This storage is necessary to provide the service you ask for, so it does not require consent (Art. 22.2 LSSI, the Spanish Information Society Services Act). You can delete the model from the app (“Model” → “Delete downloaded model”), or delete everything from your browser settings.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, rectification, erasure, objection to, restriction of and portability of your data by writing to the email address above. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). As there are no accounts, we will normally not be able to link an IP address in the logs to a person.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its new date. If analytics or non-essential cookies were ever added, your consent would be requested first.
