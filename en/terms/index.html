What it is

Anonimiza is a free tool that runs in your browser. It looks for personal data in a text using rules and the GLiNER language model, lets you review what it finds and replaces it with markers. It can also put the original data back into a text that contains those markers.

Your responsibility

  • Always review the result. Automatic detection is not perfect: it may leave personal data unreplaced, and it does not take into account data that identifies someone through context (job titles, dates, places, circumstances).
  • You are responsible for the texts you process and for what you do with the result, including sharing it with third-party services such as AI assistants.
  • Use the tool only with texts you have the right to process, and in accordance with the data protection rules that apply to you.

No warranty

The tool is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind: not that it will detect every item of personal data, not that the result will be anonymous for legal purposes, and not that it will be available at all times. A text with markers that can be restored is generally pseudonymised data, not anonymous data.
